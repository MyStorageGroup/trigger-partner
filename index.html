Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

How and when do I get paid? expand_more

Commissions are paid via direct bank transfer within 7 business days of your referred client signing their storage contract. You'll receive a payment confirmation email each time.

How do I track my referrals? expand_more

Every partner receives a dedicated partner dashboard showing real-time status for each referral — from first contact to signed contract to payment issued. You'll also receive email notifications at each stage so you're never left wondering.

What if my client forgets to mention my name? expand_more

No referral is ever lost. Each partner is issued a unique tracking link and partner code. When your client uses your link, the referral is recorded automatically — regardless of whether they mention your name. We also accept manual attribution requests within 30 days of a client signing.

Is there a minimum number of referrals required? expand_more

No minimum. Start earning from your very first referral. There are no setup fees, no monthly fees, and no commitment. If you send one client a year or fifty, you get paid for every one that converts.

Are there additional perks beyond the cash commission? expand_more

Yes. Active partners also get access to co-branded marketing materials, priority support from a dedicated partner manager, and early access to new MyStorage services and locations — giving you more value to offer your own clients.
